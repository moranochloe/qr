01 / THE BRIEFPROJECT OVERVIEW

A QR reader
that gets
out of the way.

Scanline turns a camera or image into a readable QR code, right in your browser.

Our goal is to make scanning feel straightforward: give the browser camera access, point it at a code, and see the decoded result. Prefer not to use a camera? Upload a saved image instead.

APIQRServer · Read QR Code
Free image-decoding endpoint, no API key. We send a captured or selected image and display its JSON response.↗
